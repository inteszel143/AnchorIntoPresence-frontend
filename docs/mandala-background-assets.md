# Mandala background assets

Created with the built-in image generation tool. These standalone 1024 × 1536 PNGs combine clouds and a faint mandala; opacity is visually approximated, not an exact alpha measurement. They are not yet wired into the onboarding screen.

- `assets/images/onboarding/mandala-background-light.png`
- `assets/images/onboarding/mandala-background-dark.png`

Inputs: the matching `app-background-light.png` / `app-background-dark.png` and `golden-stillness-petals-light.png` / `golden-stillness-petals.png`.

## Prompts

Light:

Use case: compositing. Create a portrait 1024x1536 mobile onboarding BACKGROUND asset, no text or UI. Image 1 is the cloud background to preserve; image 2 is the gold mandala to incorporate. Preserve image 1's warm ivory cream palette and soft cloud texture. Composite a single complete mandala from image 2 horizontally centered, center at 36% of canvas height, diameter 78% of canvas width. Mandala must be VERY FAINT, visually equivalent to 15% opacity over the cloud background, quiet low contrast watermark, never bright gold or a focal graphic. Preserve its petal geometry. Lower 40% remains open background for subtitle and buttons. Output one finished opaque PNG with clouds and faint mandala baked together. No borders, text, icons, phones or checkerboard.

Dark:

Use case: compositing. Create a portrait 1024x1536 mobile onboarding BACKGROUND asset, no text or UI. Image 1 is the cloud background to preserve; image 2 is the gold mandala to incorporate. Preserve image 1's deep warm charcoal brown palette and soft cloud texture. Composite a single complete mandala from image 2 horizontally centered, center at 36% of canvas height, diameter 78% of canvas width. Mandala must be VERY FAINT, visually equivalent to 15% opacity over the cloud background, quiet low contrast watermark, never bright gold or a focal graphic. Preserve its petal geometry. Lower 40% remains open background for subtitle and buttons. Output one finished opaque PNG with clouds and faint mandala baked together. No borders, text, icons, phones or checkerboard.
